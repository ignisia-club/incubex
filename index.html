8-STEP LAUNCHPAD TIMELINE

Follow the structured chronological milestones from national registration on Unstop to the Grand Finale at MIT-WPU Pune.

25 Sept – 2 Oct 2026

Step 01 • Phase 1
Registration & Outreach

Team Enrolment on Unstop

Assemble your team (1–6 members for Product Track, 3–6 for Prototype Expo) and register on the official Unstop portal. Teams pay the initial flat ₹200 registration fee to secure their participation.

Required Deliverable: Provide college ID credentials and select track & category.
Immediate Milestone: Unlocks download access to the official 10-slide INCUBEX blueprint template.

Early Oct 2026

Step 02 • Blueprint Build
Preparation & Architecture

Project Formulation & 10-Slide Deck Building

Teams draft their comprehensive presentation according to the official INCUBEX 10-slide standard blueprint covering problem validation, technical schematics, market viability, and system architecture.

Focus Areas: Hardware BOM, block diagrams, software code/CAD repo links, and customer pain point validation.
Demo Video: Record a 3-minute video showing the functional hardware prototype circuit or software walkthrough.

2 Oct 2026

Strict Cutoff (11:59 PM)
National Submission Deadline

Online Submission Cutoff on Unstop

Strict nationwide digital submission deadline on Unstop. Teams must upload their presentation deck, functional demo video link, and repository/schematic preview before midnight.

Cutoff Time: 11:59 PM IST sharp. Portal closes automatically. Late entries will not be admitted.
Screening Ingestion: All submissions are batched and routed to the faculty and industry screening jury.

6 – 7 Oct 2026

Step 04 • Virtual Defense
Qualifier Round

Online Qualifier Round & Virtual Jury Defense

Shortlisted teams present virtually to expert screening panels composed of academic evaluators, university faculty, and industry CTOs. Conducted 100% online to facilitate pan-India participation without travel friction.

Defense Timing: 7-minute pitch presentation + 5-minute technical Q&A defense before panels.
Jury Rubric: Scored across Problem Need (25%), Tech Feasibility (25%), Novelty (25%), and Market Impact (25%).

9 Oct 2026

Step 05 • 80 Finalists
National Shortlist

Official Declaration of 80 Finalists

The nationwide shortlist is declared: strictly 30 Product Track teams and 50 Prototype Track teams advance to the Grand Finale at MIT-WPU Pune. Selected teams pay the ₹600 finalist fee to confirm on-campus booth infrastructure and amenities.

Finalist Pass (₹600): Confirms designated exhibition booth, food passes, electricity points, and MIT-WPU security entry pass.
Official Documents: Formal university leave recommendation letters issued to help outstation teams secure college leave.

15 – 18 Oct 2026

Step 06 • Mentorship
Clinic & Polish

Prototype Readiness & Pitch Deck Finalization

Finalist teams undergo virtual mentoring clinic sessions with domain mentors to fine-tune physical hardware rigs, review cloud uptime, ensure safety compliance, and rehearse their 7-minute stage pitch.

Hardware Testing: Review sensor calibrations, mechanical casings, power supplies, and battery specs.
Presentation Coaching: 1-on-1 feedback on executive pitch narrative and live stage demo pacing.

21 Oct 2026

The Grand Finale Day
MIT-WPU Pune Campus • In-Person Expo

The Grand Finale & National Technology Expo

Full-day national exhibition on the historic MIT-WPU Kothrud campus in Pune. 80 physical exhibition booths, live walk-around judging, investor pitches in the auditorium, live delegates, and the grand awards gala.

Auditorium Keynote Stage & Live Jury Rounds • MIT-WPU Pune
Morning Schedule: 8:30 AM Booth Setup → 10:00 AM Inauguration → 11:00 AM Walk-around Jury Rounds across all 80 booths.
Afternoon & Evening: 2:30 PM Top 10 Stage Pitching in front of VCs & Angels → 5:00 PM Cash Awards & Valedictory Ceremony.

Post 21 Oct 2026

Step 08 • Evolve & Scale
Incubation & Acceleration

Incubation, Seed Support & Network Access

Winners and standout projects receive fast-track incubation support at MIT-WPU Technology Business Incubator (TBI), direct investor syndicate introductions, patent consultation, national merit certificates, and cash prize disbursements.

Ongoing Mentorship: Access to Ignisia's alumni network, venture capital partner office hours, and technical labs.
Venture Fast-Track: Direct pitch routing to institutional venture funds and state/central government grant programs.
